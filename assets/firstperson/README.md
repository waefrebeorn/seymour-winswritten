# First-Person Source — YOUR History (the real toilet-book content)

This is the safest, highest-value stash: your own gaming history, internet moments, and
meme facts. Your copyright. Zero licensing exposure. The PD clipart/fonts are just the
90s dressing; THIS is what people buy the book for.

## Capture flow
1. On stream, hit deliberate keyword markers (see `keyword_list.md`) so the absorber
   tags moments cleanly.
2. Absorber pulls transcript → `moments/<topic>_<date>.md` raw.
3. TDA triple-audit the moment (fact / structure / trap).
4. Rewrite as Uncle John entry (hook-first, one story, sidebar + footnotes, kicker).
5. Pair with a dead-to-rights CC0 clipart from `../clipart/`.

## Why this beats scraping
- No copyright risk. Ever.
- Voice is already yours — lower slop score than any LLM draft.
- Gaming/internet history IS the niche. "Wikipedia our own history" = footnoted,
  cross-ref'd, but told like a weird uncle, not an encyclopedia.
