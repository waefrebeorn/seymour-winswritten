# Stream Capture Prompts — Seymour Ponderables Vol 1
*Say these on stream. The absorber (SeymourAbsorber/1.0) grabs anything tagged and files it to `assets/firstperson/moments/`. Your words = your copyright = zero licensing exposure.*

## How to fire it
Just say the keyword out loud, then riff. That's it. No special command.
- `[PONDERABLE: <topic>]` — flags a moment for that entry
- `[GAME:]` — a gaming history fact/moment
- `[NET:]` — an internet history moment
- `[MEME-FACT:]` — a meme you're explaining

The absorber filters OUT pure gaming-noise (you playing) and KEEPS the meta moment (you talking about gaming). So narrate the *story*, not the gameplay.
