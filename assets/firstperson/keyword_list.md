# Stream Keyword Markers — for clean absorb + TDA

Say these (or type in chat) so the absorber tags moments without gaming-noise filtering:

## Core Ponderable tags (say out loud on stream)
- `[PONDERABLE: penny]` — any penny/mom/wife/found-a-penny moment
- `[PONDERABLE: versa]` — commute, drive, gas, traffic, road thought
- `[PONDERABLE: cuda]` / `[PONDERABLE: bytropix]` — kernel, GPU, inference, bug fix
- `[PONDERABLE: paulsen]` / `[PONDERABLE: hatchet]` — books read, survival, foster
- `[PONDERABLE: supercap]` — car, EV, hybrid, generator, redundancy
- `[PONDERABLE: wubu]` — nesting, hyperbolic, math, proof
- `[PONDERABLE: meme]` — a meme's origin / death / what it means
- `[PONDERABLE: internet]` — an internet-history moment, old site, dead platform

## Gaming-history moments (your primary first-person content)
- `[GAME: <name>]` — when you played it, what it meant, a story
- `[GAME-MOMENT: <name>]` — one specific moment worth an entry
- `[INTERNET: <year/thing>]` — dial-up, forum, IRC, early YouTube, dead site

## Meme facts (for the "Wikipedia our own history" angle)
- `[MEME-FACT: <meme>]` — where it came from, when it died, why it mattered
- `[DEAD-MEME: <meme>]` — extinct meme worth an obituary entry

## Why keyword
The older pipeline needed aggressive gaming-noise filtering (penny=Marvel char,
versa=gaming chat). Explicit markers remove that guesswork — the absorber grabs the
RIGHT moment, not the noise. One marker per story beat is enough.
