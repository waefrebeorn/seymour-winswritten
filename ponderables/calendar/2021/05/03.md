**FACT:** 2000: The sport of geocaching begins, with the first cache placed and the coordinates from a GPS posted on Usenet.¹
It's a people story, and people are the original meme — we copy each other faster than we copy code. History is the fossil record of which copies survived. The Cambrian explosion of 2020 proved the rule: when the environment changes fast, the copies that propagate are the ones built for speed, not truth.

> **COLONEL (codec transmission):** The web was supposed to catch facts. It caught you instead.

The fossil record states it plainly above. The folklore record — what people *felt* about it — is a different document, written six weeks later in group chat, mutated by the feed.
