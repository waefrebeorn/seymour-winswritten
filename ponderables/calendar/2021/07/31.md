**FACT:** 1715: Seven days after a Spanish treasure fleet of 12 ships left Havana, Cuba for Spain, 11 of them sink in a storm off the coast of Florida. A few centuries later, treasure is salvaged from these wrecks.¹
The copper thread again. Under the memetic lens, it's a transaction of meaning disguised as a transaction of money. The price moves; the *story* about the price moves faster. Seymour's law: a penny is the only coin small enough that everybody ignores it and significant enough that nobody forgets finding one heads-up.

> **COLONEL (codec transmission):** The smallest unit is the one the system ignores — that is where control lives.

The fossil record states it plainly above. The folklore record — what people *felt* about it — is a different document, written six weeks later in group chat, mutated by the feed.
