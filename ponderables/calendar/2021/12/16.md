**FACT:** 1838: Great Trek: Battle of Blood River: Voortrekkers led by Andries Pretorius and Sarel Cilliers defeat Zulu impis, led by Dambuza (Nzobo) and Ndlela kaSompisi in what is today KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.¹
It's a wilderness story. Paulsen's lesson: the terrain doesn't negotiate. A machine promises you've beaten nature; nature collects. The lockdown was a wilderness of the living room — we rediscovered that four walls are also terrain, and they also charge rent.
