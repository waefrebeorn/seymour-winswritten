**FACT:** 1969: Space Race: Soviet spacecraft Soyuz 4 and Soyuz 5 perform the first-ever docking of crewed spacecraft in orbit, the first-ever transfer of crew from one space vehicle to another, and the only time such a transfer was accomplished with a space walk.¹
It's a movement story. The Versa gets ~40mpg and the commute is 1.5 hours each way — three hours of life per day spent *between* places, which is exactly where the mind does its best wandering. Every vehicle is a thesis about how much of your day you're willing to sell to travel. The crash, the road, the traffic: the terrain collects its rent.

> **COLONEL (codec transmission):** Three hours a day spent between places — that is the rent the terrain collects.
