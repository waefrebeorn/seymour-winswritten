## Preamble

The populace is free. The corporation is an entity of money. We scale by definition to allow small businesses, governments, non-profits, and educational entities to use freely and stop being taken advantage of by mega-corporations.

This license is the master license for ALL Waefrebeorn projects across ALL media, repos, and platforms. It is broad, agnostic, and designed to maximize freedom for the general population while ensuring that governments, entities, and large commercial actors integrate responsibly.

We disassociate ourselves from malicious compliance and misuse. We cannot control what every user does with our creations, because we are software creators, not moral policemen. But we retain the right to deny our endorsement, cooperation, and assistance to those who use our work for non-moral and non-allowable activities. That is not a license restriction — it is a dispensation: we choose not to be responsible for what we cannot control.

The Licensor explicitly disclaims responsibility for the use of Waefrebeorn Work by third parties. The Licensor is a software creator, not a moral authority over how end users apply the tools. The Licensor does not control downstream deployment, integration, or use. This dispensation covers:

The Dispensation is NOT a license grant — it is a statement that the Licensor does not bear downstream responsibility for uses it cannot predict or control.

## 11. Modifiable License Reserve (Informed by "Modifying OSS Licenses" Research)

Drawing from the academic research paper "Modifying OSS Licenses" (Gagnon, Benjamin, Régis, et al., 2024 — `arxiv.org/pdf/2407.13064`), the Licensor reserves the right to:

### 11.1 Modification Provisions
- The Licensor may modify this license to add new restrictions or clarify existing ones through a publicly documented governance process
- Modifications apply only to NEW distributions of Waefrebeorn Work — existing distributions and their derivatives continue under the version under which they were received

### 11.2 Revocation Reserve
- In extreme cases (e.g., Waefrebeorn Work being used in a way that causes demonstrable, widespread, irreversible harm that the Licensor did not anticipate), the Licensor reserves the right to issue a modification that restricts or revokes permissions for specific use cases
- Such modifications must be made in good faith, applied uniformly, and include a sufficient remediation period (minimum 12 months)

### 11.3 Qualification
- Modifications must apply uniformly to all downstream recipients of the same version
- Modifications must be made in good faith and not discriminate against specific users or groups
- Modifications must be publicly documented with clear rationale
- Revocation or restriction must be a last resort, not a routine tool

### 11.4 Purpose
These provisions exist because: (a) AI models can cause harm that contributors cannot predict at release time; (b) the legal environment around AI is evolving rapidly; (c) open source AI contributors need the ability to respond to emergent harms while maintaining the trust of the community. This provision is modeled on the "modifiable license" framework proposed by Gagnon et al. (2024) and adapted for the Waefrebeorn use case.
