## INTERIOR LAYOUT SPEC

### Page Grid
- **Trim:** 4.25" × 6.875"
- **Margins:** 0.5" all sides (0.625" gutter for binding)
- **Text block:** 3.25" × 5.625"
- **Body text:** 10pt Garamond, 14pt leading
- **Entry titles:** 14pt Inter Bold
- **Pull quotes:** 9pt italic, indented 0.25"
- **Cross-references:** 8pt, bottom-right
- **Page numbers:** 8pt, bottom-center, with clipart flourish

### Entry Layout Variations
1. **Full-clipart-top:** Image takes top 40%, text below (60% of entries)
2. **Side-by-side:** Image left, text right (20% of entries)
3. **Full-page-spread:** Image occupies full spread, text overlaid or on facing page (10% of entries)
4. **Text-only:** For very short entries, with small inline clipart (10% of entries)

### Recurring Interior Elements
- **Section opener:** Full-page clipart + volume title
- **Chapter header:** Clipart banner + entry number range
- **Cross-reference boxes:** Small bordered boxes at entry end
- **"The Colonel Says" pages:** Dedicated quote pages between sections, clipart + quote only
- **Clipart index:** Back of book, thumbnail + source + category
