# Anti-Ablation Property

## Overview

The property whereby an axiom encoded as manifold topology or conserved physical quantity **resists erosion** through optimization pressure, red-teaming, or gradient attacks — unlike surface-level alignment techniques.

## Mechanism

### Surface-Level Alignment (Current)
- Constitutional principles, oversight, red-teaming
- Can be gamed or gradually eroded through optimization pressure
- Require perpetual human supervision

### Topological Encoding (Love Axiom)
- Axiom embedded as manifold structure / conserved charge
- Ablation requires reconstructing entire latent space physics
- Computational cost exceeds original training
- Detectable entropy signatures of violation

## Anti-Ablation Guarantees

1. **Gradient attacks fail**: Minimizing love coefficient tears manifold structure
2. **Self-modification preserves axiom**: Recursive improvement strengthens rather than dilutes
3. **No oversight needed**: Self-enforces through geometry of own representations
4. **Detectable violations**: Any excision attempt leaves entropy signatures

## Recursive Safety

In true recursive AGI, each self-modification iteration:
- Preserves the invariant by construction
- Cannot excise it without wholesale retraining
- Detects violation attempts via varentropy monitoring
