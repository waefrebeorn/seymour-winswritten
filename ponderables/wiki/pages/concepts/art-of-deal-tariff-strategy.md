# Art of the Deal Tariff Strategy

## Overview

The tariff negotiation approach characterized by: high initial demands, selective pauses, bilateral frameworks, and structured concession extraction — as demonstrated in the April-July 2025 U.S.-Japan episode.

## Core Pattern

1. **Aggressive Opening** — Maximum pressure (IEEPA baseline 10%, Japan-specific 25% threat)
2. **Selective Pauses** — 75-country pause signals flexibility without weakness
3. **Bilateral Engagement** — Direct talks with key allies (Japan prioritized)
4. **Framework Agreement** — Structured reciprocity: tariffs ↔ investments/purchases
5. **No Piecemeal Exemptions** — Avoids sector-specific lobbying erosion

## Results (U.S.-Japan July 2025)

- **15% baseline tariff** (down from 25% threat)
- **$550B Japanese investment** in U.S. strategic sectors through 2029
- **Significant U.S. purchases** (aircraft, defense, ag, energy)
- **Precedent for reciprocity** without sector exceptions

## Contrast with Nintendo Exclusion

| Nintendo Carve-Out | Framework Agreement |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Short-term populist win | Structural leverage |
| Risks tariff wall credibility | Establishes reciprocity precedent |
| Invites sector lobbying | Comprehensive concessions |
| Symbolic | Substantive |
