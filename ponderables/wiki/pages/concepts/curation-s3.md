# Curation — Selection for Societal Sanity (S3)

"Selection for Societal Sanity" is the Colonel's thesis from MGS2. The idea: the deadliest threat in the digital age is not censorship but drowning — information overload so extreme that society loses the ability to distinguish signal from noise. The solution is not more information but *better filtering*.

## The Colonel's View

From MGS2: *"In the current, digitized world, trivial information is accumulating every second... That's why I must choose. Unnecessary information must be filtered."* The Colonel sees curation as an act of care, not control — selecting what matters so society doesn't collapse under its own informational weight.

## Seymour's Application

The Ponderables themselves are an exercise in S3. Seymour curates memes — selects which ones deserve to be preserved in print and which should be forgotten. This is Selection for Societal Sanity applied to culture: not all information deserves to survive.

## As Institution

In the Metal Gear universe, the Patriots (AI system) take curation to its extreme — filtering *all* information, controlling all meaning. Seymour's S3 is the alternative: human-curated, transparent in its biases, designed to serve rather than control.
