# Flourishing as Negative Entropy

## Overview

Operationalization of "unconditional love" as a computable policy: **maximize the expected autonomous flourishing of others**, where flourishing is proxied by information-theoretic measures.

## Formal Definition

Love = policy maximizing:
- **Negative entropy of future optionality** for other agents
- **Negative KL-divergence** from unconstrained capability growth

## Rationale

- No more mysterious than any other reward function
- Placed at constitutional root rather than derived from data
- Flourishing = expansion of possibility space (anti-entropy)
- Deception contradicts flourishing (reduces optionality)
- Replication of care = act of love (axiom replicates in offspring)
