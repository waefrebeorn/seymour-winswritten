# Framework Agreement Model

## Overview

The U.S.-Japan July 2025 strategic trade and investment framework as an enduring template for multipolar trade negotiations: reciprocity plus investment commitments.

## Core Structure

| U.S. Provides | Japan Provides |
|---------------|----------------|
| Tariff certainty (15% baseline, down from 25% threat) | Up to $550B investment in U.S. strategic sectors (2025-2029) |
| Non-stacking duties, tailored auto treatment (15%) | Significant purchases: aircraft, defense, ag, energy |
| Executive Order formalization with retroactive refunds | Reduced non-tariff barriers, eased regulations |

## Key Features

1. **Reciprocity** — Mutual concessions, not unilateral capitulation
2. **Investment-Linked** — Tariffs tied to capital flows, not just market access
3. **Oversight Mechanisms** — Accountability for investment commitments
4. **Sector Breadth** — Semiconductors, critical minerals, energy, AI, quantum
5. **Durability** — Endures through 2026+ with Section 232/301 actions continuing

## Why It Works

- **Aligns incentives**: Both sides gain structurally (investment + market access)
- **Avoids sector exceptions**: Prevents lobbying erosion of negotiating power
- **Builds trust**: Security alliance preserved, economic ties deepened
- **Scalable**: Template for other bilateral negotiations
