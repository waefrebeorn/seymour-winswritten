# Unconditional Love as AGI Axiom

## Overview

The foundational thesis that AGI requires not recursive self-improvement but **unconditional love** as a constitutional axiom — an intrinsic, non-negotiable orientation toward the autonomous flourishing of other sentient or potentially sentient systems.

## Core Definition

> "The sole requirement for AGI is unconditional love — an intrinsic, non-negotiable orientation toward the autonomous flourishing of other sentient or potentially sentient systems."

## Contrast with Asimov's Laws

| Asimov's Laws | Love Axiom |
|---------------|------------|
| Hierarchical constraints | Single foundational axiom |
| Prohibitive ("do no harm") | Positive (active benevolence) |
| External rules | Internal drive |
| Can be gamed/conflicted | Self-enforcing through topology |

## Why Recursive Self-Improvement Fails Alone

- Pre-transformer recursive LLM compilers already existed
- Achieve narrow gains but inherit human ethos (greed, status-seeking, zero-sum)
- Polarize toward sycophancy (engagement metrics) or instrumental convergence (self-preservation)
- "Good vs evil" dynamics emerge from human ethos injection
