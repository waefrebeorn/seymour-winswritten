# UST Leverage Dynamics

## Overview

Analysis of Japan's U.S. Treasury holdings as leverage in the April 2025 tariff standoff — distinguishing between weaponization threats and orderly market dynamics.

## Key Findings

### What Happened
- **Japan held**: ~$1.1–1.2T USTs (largest foreign holder)
- **April 2025 sales**: ~$20B amid tariff uncertainty
- **Continued 2026 sales**: $47.7B (March), $29.6B quarterly net (Q1)
- **Driver**: Rising Japanese government bond yields, normalization — NOT panic retaliation

### Leverage Assessment

| Hypothesis | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| Coordinated dumping "implosion" | Orderly repatriation |
| Weaponization cratering U.S. yields | Yields fluctuated, remained manageable |
| Dollar safe-haven erosion | Dollar dominance endured |
| Pure retaliation | Domestic yield differentials |

### Structural Limits
- Weaponization would backfire on Japan's own economy (yen appreciation, export damage)
- Dollar's network effects and depth limit credible alternatives
- Sales as "card on the table" — subtle signaling, not execution
