# Bessent Market Stability

## Overview

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's role in the April 2025 tariff standoff — prioritizing market stability, inflation optics, and UST dynamics management.

## Key Actions

- Central role in U.S.-Japan negotiations, prioritizing Tokyo talks
- Focus on comprehensive deal tying tariffs to investment commitments
- Managed UST dynamics: Japanese sales were orderly repatriation ($20B April 2025, continued through 2026), not implosion
- 15% tariff cap achieved stabilization without precedent-setting carve-outs

## Nintendo Exclusion Assessment

- Nintendo-specific exclusion might have signaled flexibility and calmed consumer prices
- But 15% cap achieved similar stabilization without precedent-setting carve-outs
- Pragmatism over symbolic gestures — behind-the-scenes negotiation focus
