# Japan UST Holder

## Overview

Japan as the largest foreign holder of U.S. Treasuries (~$1.1–1.2 trillion) during the April 2025 tariff standoff — analyzed for leverage dynamics and market impact.

## UST Dynamics

| Period | Net Sales | Driver |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| April 2025 | ~$20 billion | Tariff uncertainty, yen defense |
| March 2026 | $47.7 billion | Rising JGB yields, normalization |
| Q1 2026 | $29.6 billion quarterly net | Continued orderly repatriation |

## Leverage Assessment

- **Not weaponization**: Sales driven by domestic yield differentials (rising JGB yields), not pure retaliation
- **"Card on the table"**: Japanese officials subtly referenced holdings as leverage
- **No implosion**: Orderly repatriation, U.S. yields fluctuated but remained manageable
- **Dollar dominance endured**: Despite pressure, safe-haven status held
