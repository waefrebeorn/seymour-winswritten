# Musk Trade Stance

## Overview

Elon Musk's position during the April 2025 tariff standoff — publicly opposed broad tariffs, no documented Nintendo-specific advocacy.

## Key Positions

- **April 2025**: Publicly warned of distortions, retaliation risks to Tesla, potential recessionary effects
- **Priorities**: DOGE efficiency drives, EV supply chains, SpaceX, not Nintendo
- **Tensions**: Public friction with administration elements (including Bessent)
- **Indirect benefit**: Stable Japan deal aligned with national strategy (critical minerals investments)

## Nintendo Exclusion Assessment

- No involvement in Nintendo-specific advocacy
- Gaming affinity (Tesla arcade, personal enjoyment) but peripheral to trade priorities
- Tesla Shanghai exports affected by China tariffs (125%) — direct supply-chain concern
- Any Japan deal benefit came via strategic minerals investments, not Nintendo
