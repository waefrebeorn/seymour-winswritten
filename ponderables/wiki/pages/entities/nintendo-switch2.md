# Nintendo Switch 2

## Overview

Nintendo's next-generation console launched June 5, 2025 at $450, navigating the April 2025 tariff environment without a bespoke exclusion.

## Launch Performance

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Launch Date | June 5, 2025 |
| Price | $450 (held despite tariffs) |
| First 4 Days | 3.5 million units |
| First 4 Months | 10.36 million units |
| Target (March 2026) | 19 million units |

## Tariff Impact

- Initial forecasts assumed ~10% tariff rates
- Vietnam manufacturing adjustments pushed effective rates higher
- Company absorbed some costs, raised accessory prices modestly
- **No dedicated Nintendo exclusion** — broader Japan framework capped Japanese-sourced duties at 15%
- Demand resilient: tariffs "pulled some demand forward" without collapsing category
- No massive gray-market subversion at scale (logistics, warranties, region-free software mitigated)
