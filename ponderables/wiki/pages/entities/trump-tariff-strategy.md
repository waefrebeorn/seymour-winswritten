# Trump Tariff Strategy

## Overview

President Trump's April 2025 tariff actions under IEEPA — baseline 10% on most imports, 25% threatened on Japan, with broader pressures on China and Vietnam. Core strategy: projecting strength, rebalancing trade, winning optics.

## Art of the Deal Approach

1. **High initial demands** — Create urgency and leverage
2. **Selective pauses** — 75-country pause, signals flexibility without weakness
3. **Bilateral frameworks** — Japan deal: 15% baseline, $550B investment commitments
4. **Concession extraction** — Structured reciprocity over piecemeal exemptions

## Nintendo Exclusion Assessment

- **Short-term**: Populist appeal ("saved Mario"), gaming goodwill
- **Risk**: Dilutes tariff wall credibility, invites sector lobbying (autos, steel)
- **Outcome**: No bespoke exclusion; broader framework delivered superior leverage
- **Validation**: Switch 2 strong sales proved consumers absorbed costs
