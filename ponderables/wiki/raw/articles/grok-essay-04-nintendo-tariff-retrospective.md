Automobiles and parts received tailored 15% treatment; aerospace, generic pharmaceuticals, and select natural resources saw favorable or zero additional duties.

In exchange, Japan committed to:
Significant purchases of U.S. aircraft, defense equipment, agricultural goods, and energy.
Up to $550 billion in investments in strategic U.S. sectors (semiconductors, critical minerals, energy, AI, quantum) through 2029, with oversight mechanisms.
Reduced non-tariff barriers and eased regulations for U.S. firms.

An Executive Order in September 2025 formalized the tariff structure, applying retroactively to August 7, 2025, with refunds where overpayments occurred.

Yields fluctuated but remained manageable; the dollar's dominance endured. Bessent played a central role in negotiations, prioritizing Tokyo and helping craft the framework that stabilized relations.
