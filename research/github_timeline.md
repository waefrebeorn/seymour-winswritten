- **2024 H2–2025 H1:** steady multi-project cadence (systems, tooling, experiments).
- **2025 H2 takeoff:** the LLM-assisted workflow kicks in — `slermes` (pure C11 Hermes reimplementation) and `WuBuMath` emerge; commit volume doubles.
- **2026:** the curve goes vertical. `slermes` (47MB single binary, 352 .c files), the Devil's-Advocate audit forks (`eshkol_audit`, `quantum_rng_audit`), and the Seymour Ponderables wiki all land in a tight burst.
- **The genius move:** not just *more* commits, but *verifiable* ones — every audit claim is build-and-run proven, every PR body references only repo-internal paths, every fix shipped upstream (PR #275, PR #6). Effort converted into reproducible evidence.
