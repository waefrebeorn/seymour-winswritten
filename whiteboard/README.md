## HOW THIS WORKS

1. You talk → Siri → Telegram → arrives here
2. I log it RAW in `whiteboard/raw-inbox.md` (no editing, no judgment)
3. When you say "organize" or "process", I move captures into `whiteboard/organized/`
4. Organized captures get tagged and cross-referenced
5. When a book has enough captures, we draft from them

**This is the tape deck. Record everything. Organize later.**
